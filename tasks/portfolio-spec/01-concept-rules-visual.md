# Portfolio spec: §1 Concept, §2 Content and privacy rules, §3 Visual system

Part of `tasks/portfolio-spec/` (index: `README.md`).

## 1. Concept

The site is a **live football broadcast of Vishal's career**: scorebug, team sheet, match timeline, highlights with "VAR reviews", a pundit studio, and full time. It is positioned as **AI/ML engineer first** (computer vision, self-retraining pipelines, generative data), product builder second (PrintSafe).

Three ideas carry it:
1. **Show, don't tell.** Every project card is playable: a demo that behaves like the real thing.
2. **Pixel-art Vishal.** The only image of him on the site is a pixel sprite. It blinks, glances, and follows the cursor.
3. **Broadcast craft.** Floodlights for the theme switch, a ticker, a whistle, crowd noise, and scroll moments in the style of Apple product pages.

## 2. Content and privacy rules (non-negotiable)

- No real photos of Vishal. Only the pixel sprite and the pixel shirts. The one real photo is the **marathon medal** (no person in it).
- No photos that show other people's faces (race crowd, team photos, medal ceremony). Use them as reference only.
- Never publish phone numbers, recommenders' names, emails or phone numbers, or LinkedIn analytics (impressions, profile views).
- Recommendation-letter quotes are **removed**. Only reintroduce one with that recommender's explicit permission.
- Heart-rate data from the run is **not shown**.
- "Open to work" (recruiters-only on LinkedIn) and grad-school plans are **not shown** unless Vishal asks.
- Shirt number is **4** everywhere.
- No invented numbers. Every stat on the page must trace to the resume, LinkedIn, a project README, or Vishal.

| green (checks) | `#6fd08c` | `#1f7a3c` |
| blue (elephant print) | `#8fb0d9` | `#8fb0d9` |

- **Accent:** floodlight yellow `#f5d547`. Mock alternatives: `#8fb0d9` and `#ff8a3d`. Text on the accent is always `#0b1610`.
- **Theme:** dark by default, with a light toggle saved to `localStorage`. This deliberately overrides the usual "light default" convention for this project.
- **Kit room panel:** always a dark showroom (`#070c0a`) in both themes.

### Type

| Role | Font | Weights |
|---|---|---|
| Display | Barlow Condensed | 600, 700, 800 (uppercase headlines) |
| Body | Barlow | 400, 500, 600 |
| Labels, HUD, scorebug details | Silkscreen (pixel font) | 400, 700 |

### Shape

- Neo-brutalist: 2px solid borders and hard offset shadows (`5px 5px 0`, `8px 8px 0` on big panels), no blur.
- Buttons nudge on hover (−2px) and press in on click (+2px).
- Icons are inline stroke SVGs. No emoji anywhere, including arrow glyphs that render as emoji on iOS.
- Touch targets are at least 44px. Text contrast passes 4.5:1.

### Pixel art

- Draw with `image-rendering: pixelated` and scale by whole numbers (the PNGs are exported at 8x).
- Every sprite is generated by a script, so edits are reproducible (see §8).
