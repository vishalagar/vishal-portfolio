Street Rescue · 90 seconds

Golden hour patrol

Shoo the rowdies before they swing, then lead the dogs to the van. Three scared-off dogs ends the round.

How a rescue works

  1. A rowdy walks up to a dog and raises a stick. You see a ! over his head.
  2. Get close and Shoo. He tumbles over and runs off.
  3. In a crowd, Cookie barks: everyone near her freezes for a moment.
  4. Walk into a dog to clip on a lead (two at a time), then walk to the van.
